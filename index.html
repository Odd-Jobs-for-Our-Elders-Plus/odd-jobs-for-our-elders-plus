Home Safety & Maintenance Check

Sometimes the most valuable service is simply having another set of eyes walk through the home.

$100

One Visit. One Flat Fee.

For just $100, get greater peace of mind with a thorough Home Safety & Maintenance Check! 💚

Whether you're helping an aging loved one remain independent, preparing a home for a new baby, moving into a new place, or simply wanting to stay ahead of household maintenance, this standalone service is designed to help you identify potential concerns before they become bigger problems.

For one flat fee of $100, I'll walk through your home with you, look for potential safety hazards and maintenance concerns, and provide a detailed written list of observations and recommended improvements.

Your $100 check includes:

  • 🏡 A thorough home walkthrough
  • ⚠️ Observations of potential safety hazards and fall risks
  • 🔧 Identification of visible maintenance concerns and preventative upkeep opportunities
  • ♿ Suggestions for accessibility, organization, and everyday convenience
  • 📝 A detailed written summary of findings and recommended next steps

No additional work is required. You'll receive your observations and recommendations whether you decide to tackle the improvements yourself, hire another professional, or ask me to provide a separate estimate for any work you'd like completed.

One visit. One flat fee. A little more peace of mind. 💚

A quick note about the service: This service is a general visual safety and maintenance review, not a licensed home inspection or an evaluation of structural integrity, building-code compliance, or specialized systems. It does not replace an assessment by a qualified professional where one is needed.
