Why Odd Jobs for Our Elders, Plus?

💚 A Little About Me

Hi there! My name is Tyler, and I'm the person behind Odd Jobs for Our Elders, Plus.

I started this small, locally owned side business with a simple goal: to lend a dependable helping hand to older adults, homeowners, busy families, and anyone who could use an extra set of capable hands around the house.

Sometimes it's the little things that make the biggest difference — a loose handrail, a stubborn door, a seasonal maintenance task, or that ever-growing honey-do list that never seems to get any shorter.

As a husband, dad, and nurse, I understand how important it is to feel safe, comfortable, and independent in your own home. Through my nursing experience, I've seen firsthand how everyday household hazards can contribute to preventable falls and injuries. I believe a little attention, a few practical adjustments, and some preventative maintenance can go a long way toward helping people remain safe and independent at home.

My goal is simple: provide affordable, dependable help with patience, respect, attention to detail, and a genuine willingness to make someone's day a little easier.

💚 My Mission

To provide affordable, dependable home maintenance and practical helping-hand services that promote safer homes, preserve independence, and make everyday life a little easier — one small job at a time.
