Helping Our Community, One Odd Job at a Time 💚

Hi, I'm Tyler — a husband, dad, nurse, and proud member of the local community.

After years of working in healthcare, I've learned that sometimes what people need most isn't something complicated. It's simply a caring, capable person willing to lend a helping hand.

That's the heart behind Odd Jobs for Our Elders, Plus.

I started this business with a simple mission: to help people stay safe, comfortable and independent in their own homes while taking a few things off their plate.

Whether it's a small repair, a home safety check, seasonal maintenance, putting something together, or tackling one of those "I've been meaning to get around to that" jobs — I'm here to help.

This isn't just about odd jobs. It's about neighbors helping neighbors.

My goal is to provide dependable, honest and reasonably priced help to the people in my community — especially our older neighbors and their families who may need an extra set of capable hands.

🏠 Home Safety & Maintenance Check

This is a specialized, optional service for homeowners, families and loved ones who would appreciate a thorough second set of eyes on the home.

💚 Why might you request a Home Safety & Maintenance Check?

  • 👶 Baby or toddler proofing — preparing your home for a crawling, climbing and exploring little one.
  • 👵 Aging more independently — identifying ways to make the home safer and easier to navigate as someone gets older.
  • 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Busy families — getting another set of eyes on the never-ending list of household concerns.
  • 🏡 Moving into a new home — taking a fresh look at your home's safety and maintenance needs before you get completely settled.
  • 📦 Moving out of a home — identifying small maintenance and safety items that may need attention before listing, selling or handing over the keys.
  • 🎁 A thoughtful gift for a loved one — give a parent, grandparent, family member or friend something genuinely useful: extra safety and peace of mind.
  • 🔎 "I just want someone to look around." — nothing may seem particularly wrong; you simply want another set of eyes.
  • 💚 Safety reassurance and peace of mind — sometimes the biggest benefit is simply knowing that someone took the time to look.

1. Thorough Walk-Through

I'll walk through the home with you and look for common safety, maintenance, accessibility, organization and preventative-maintenance concerns.

2. Practical Recommendations

You'll receive a comprehensive list of observations and practical recommendations for items that may need attention, improvement or monitoring.

3. Your Choice

Choose what you'd like me to handle, what you'd rather defer, what you want to address yourself, or what should be referred to another qualified professional.

🔎 What I may look for during the check

  • Trip and fall hazards
  • Loose or unstable furniture
  • Furniture anti-tip concerns
  • Bathroom safety concerns
  • Lighting and visibility
  • Smoke detector concerns
  • Carbon monoxide detector concerns
  • Smoke/CO detector batteries
  • Door, cabinet and window issues
  • Loose or worn hardware
  • Potential accessibility challenges
  • Clutter and organization concerns
  • Electrical concerns that should be evaluated by a qualified electrician
  • Areas needing routine maintenance
  • Seasonal maintenance reminders
  • Winterization and de-winterization
  • Fire prevention considerations
  • Water damage prevention considerations
  • Moisture or potential water-intrusion concerns that warrant further evaluation
  • Potential household maintenance issues
  • Items that may become larger problems if left unattended
  • Other concerns that catch my attention during the walkthrough
🏠 Home Safety & Maintenance Check — A $100 investment in peace of mind.

A thorough walk-through and written list of safety, maintenance, accessibility and preventative-maintenance observations for your home.

This specialized service can be especially helpful for aging parents, yourself or loved ones, families preparing for a new baby, parents of toddlers, people moving into or out of a home, busy families, or anyone who simply wants another set of eyes on their home.

Additional services identified during the check are not included in the $100 service fee and may be quoted separately if you'd like me to take care of them.

The Home Safety & Maintenance Check is intended to identify common household safety, maintenance and preventative-maintenance observations.
